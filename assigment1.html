Ladakh valley

Ladakh valley is a high-altitude cold desert nestled between the towering Himalayan and Karakoram mountain ranges.

    four places to visit
  1. Pangong Lake
  2. Nubra Valley
  3. Khardung La Pass
  4. Thiksey Monastry
    The activities to do at that places
  • At Pangong Lake Camp in luxury tents or cottages right along the shoreline to watch dramatic sunsets and sunrises.
  • At Nubra Valley Bacterian Camel Riding:Ride unique, double-humped camels across the high-altitude sand dunes of Hunder village.
  • At Khardung La Pass Souvenir Shopping:Visit the world's highest souvenir shop run by the Indian Army to buy commemorative t-shirts and caps.
  • Thiskey Monastry Morning Prayers:Wake up early to attend the peaceful morning assembly where monks chant and play traditional horns.
