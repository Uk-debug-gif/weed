My Favourite Food is Burger

A burger is a popular sandwich made of a cooked patty placed inside a sliced bun. It contains;

  • The Patty: The flat, round center piece. It can be made of beef, chicken, fish, or vegetables like potatoes.
  • The Bun: A soft, sliced bread roll that holds the food together.
  • Toppings: Fresh vegetables like lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and pickles.
  • Cheese & Sauces: Melted cheese, ketchup, mayonnaise, or mustard added for extra flavor.
